# Two-gang switch 67181844 pairing investigation

## Observed failure

On 2026-11-07 the owner reported that Home Assistant's single-button pairing
detected switch `67181844`, physically identified as a two-gang switch, but did
not add it. The displayed observation said its pairing signature was
unrecognized; the form also showed `Reason: None` and duplicated punctuation.

The HA specialist inspected existing status, the durable command record and
available logs on the HA gateway without issuing radio commands or changing
the network. The reported deployment was app 0.5.0 / radio 0.13. At
15:08:55 UTC (16:08:55 Europe/Warsaw), the retained session was:

- Session: `b6ff4033-31ac-4e5e-829b-d2c1e75e1d93`.
- Terminal status: `expired`; `registered: false`.
- Session error: `unsupported_device_seen`.
- Observation: device `67181844`, suffix `0002`, reason
  `unknown_pairing_payload`.
- The device was absent from the daemon registry.

The 120-second commissioning command's durable result was updated at
15:06:59.331939 UTC. The last retained discovery timestamp was
15:06:13.153332 UTC. These are recorded result/discovery times, not a recovered
packet capture or proof of the exact time the owner viewed the form.

## Initial cause and evidence limit

The classifier recognized the subject suffix but rejected the registration
payload. Its existing two-gang rule requires suffix `0002`, payload prefix
`00 02 03 05 34 01`, and final byte `00`; byte 6 may vary. A native request must
also pass CRC decoding and the existing source/subject, tag and destination
checks before producing this observation.

The exact rejected payload was not retained in the public session record or
available logs. The ZigBee specialist scanned 64 existing local pcapng files
without read errors and found no native pairing request from this device.
Historical control/announcement traffic corroborates suffix `0002`, but does
not establish its registration signature. The differing byte(s), their meaning
and a safe broader matching rule therefore remain unknown.

There is no evidence here that the switch is one-gang or that it cannot be
controlled. There is also no justification for accepting every `0002` payload,
hardcoding this device ID, or changing radio firmware.

## Display issue and follow-up

The HA form used the literal fallback `None` when no session-level error was
present. That can occur while a session is still listening, even when a device
observation already explains why it is ineligible. The precise earlier screen
state was not retained, so this explains a rendering path rather than proving
the owner saw a particular intermediate snapshot. Observation text and the
form template also both supplied terminal punctuation.

The remediation separates clearer HA session/observation wording from bounded
retention of checksum-validated pairing payload evidence during an explicit
commissioning session. Each retained observation keeps at most two distinct
signatures with their exact eight-byte payloads; a flag reports additional
variants. Known counter-only changes do not cause extra persistence writes.
Evidence survives terminal outcomes/restarts, but never restores admission
eligibility. Older records without evidence remain supported.

HA exposes this evidence in diagnostic attributes and an anonymous diagnostic
download, not in the main pairing form. Neither change relaxes admission or
automatically repeats pairing. A later operator-authorized attempt after
deployment can establish whether an additional profile is warranted.

Independent offline validation passed 63 daemon commissioning, capture,
profile, discovery and CLI tests, plus 57 HA commissioning, client, simulated
native pairing and integration tests. These cover both supported gang counts,
unknown/conflicting evidence, persistence, bounded fields, old records,
diagnostic redaction and the reported wording. Test-generated payload variants
are synthetic regressions, not recovered bytes from switch `67181844`.

No new pairing attempt, manual admission, output command, firmware update or
service restart was performed during that initial investigation. The later
owner-initiated diagnostic attempt is recorded separately below.

## Diagnostic retry: exact payload recovered

The owner subsequently retried after the authorized HA software-only diagnostic
deployment. The HA specialist inspected existing native status and the SQLite
command ledger without issuing a pairing or output command. The active
diagnostic module SHA-256 was
`e122edd6c1b01b5484040adb6897058395193fd67bf08c507d6be18860fedeca`;
the daemon reported 0.14.0, compatibility/radio 0.13 and ready status.

- Session: `aee36fdb-17af-4234-817d-1f2fa33f1097`.
- At 15:55:33.077402 UTC (16:55:33 Warsaw), the session was listening with
  six seconds remaining, no session error and `registered: false`.
- At 15:56:12.427043 UTC, the same session was expired with
  `unsupported_device_seen`; its durable terminal update was
  15:55:40.055541 UTC. The target remained absent from the registry.
- Both observations retained suffix `0002` and exact eight-byte payload
  **`000203042e003000`**, one distinct variant, truncation false, with
  `unknown_pairing_payload` as the observation reason.

The diagnostic record was saved privately as
`/tmp/livolo-ha-67181844-retry-evidence-20261107.json`. This records a
checksum-validated native commissioning observation, not a complete captured
frame. Full raw bytes, original envelope field values and repeat counts were
not retained. One retained variant is not a claim of only one reception or of
multiple independent model observations. No conflicting signature was indicated.

The exact difference is now known:

| Payload position, zero-based | Existing two-gang layout | Newly observed layout |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2 | `00 02 03` | `00 02 03` |
| 3–5 | `05 34 01` | `04 2e 00` |
| 6 | Excluded retransmission byte | Retained value `30` |
| 7 | `00` | `00` |

There is no established interpretation of bytes 3–5 as version or model fields.
Their difference explains the classifier rejection without implying a change
to the radio timing, firmware transport or two-output control format.

## Historical control corroboration and bounded recommendation

The repeated offline scan read 64 pcaps without errors and decoded 5,570 valid
packets whose subject was `67181844`. None was its native unregistered pairing
request, and no decoded payload contained `04 2e 00`. The only previously
captured native pairing signatures belonged to `46715621` (two gang) and
`66780945` (one gang). The new signature therefore relies on the explicit
diagnostic retry, not a newly discovered old pairing capture.

Existing original-EC traffic corroborates the established two-output command
and state format for this ID. All entries below use suffix `0002` in
`captured/development/original-ec-live-20261025-nrf52840.pcapng`:

| Command frame (`8c/02`) | Command payload | Native report frame (`9e/05`) | Report payload |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1254 | `010002000000002c` | 1256 | `010002000000062c` |
| 1260 | `01000300000000a5` | 1265 | `01000300000007a5` |
| 1457 | `0100010000000038` | 1463 | `0100010000000838` |
| 1953 | `0100020200000088` | 1955 | `0100020200000f88` |

These show masks `02`, `03` and `01`, including output 2 ON. Reports are native
from `67181844` to original gateway `9bf4ea38` under tag `b1`. The separate
`sonoff-query-mcu-comparison-20261024-nrf52840.pcapng` frame 1971 carries a native
report `0100010300008815`, corroborating complete state bits `03`. The
capability-like report in `ec-edge-gateway-off-20261025-nrf52840.pcapng` frame
7665 is `0002030000000600`; it corroborates the first three bytes but does not
decode the remaining pairing fields. These facts supplement the owner's
physical two-gang confirmation, rather than deriving gang count from one mask.

The classifier change adds only suffix `0002` plus exact prefix
`000203042e00` and final byte `00` as another empirical two-output profile.
The existing exclusion of byte 6 remains, as do CRC, native family/opcode,
unregistered tag, source/subject, destination, freshness, conflict and explicit
session checks. Do not hardcode the device ID, ignore all three differing bytes,
accept every `0002` request, or infer an analogous one-gang variant. Two different
fixed prefixes observed for the same identity still constitute a conflict.

The Unix specialist's 66 passing profile, discovery, commissioning and CLI tests
cover the exact retained payload, synthetic IDs
and counter variants, rejection of altered fixed bytes/final byte/suffix/length,
existing envelope and CRC exclusions, stale/restarted sessions, conflicting old
and new prefixes, and a synthetic two-output commissioning lifecycle. Admission
must echo the selected request bytes in its ACK and retain the existing fresh
locally addressed state-report confirmation gate. Synthetic envelopes and test
counter variants are not additional captured evidence.

This is an offline host classifier correction and evidence review. It does not
establish successful live pairing or local control of the new variant. No radio
firmware, retry schedule or output behavior change is justified by this finding.
