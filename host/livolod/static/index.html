Advanced device settings
Ready to pair?
  1. If migrating, remove this switch from the old gateway and keep that gateway’s pairing disabled.
  2. Choose “Save & start pairing”, then follow your switch’s documented pairing procedure.
  3. Wait for a device report to confirm pairing. The window closes after 2 minutes.

Forget device?

The switch may retain its association. To join another gateway, use the model’s documented pairing or reset procedure.
